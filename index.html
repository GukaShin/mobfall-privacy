Privacy Policy

Mobfall · Effective 23 November 2026

Short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Mobfall ("the game") is a free game for Android (on Google Play) and the web browser. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it, in the Android app or on the website.

Information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in. There is no chat, no multiplayer and no way to contact other players.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game uses local storage on your device (localStorage and sessionStorage, inside the app or your browser). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. It contains:

  • game progress: unlocked maps, stars, hero level, talents, crystals, collected looks and pets;
  • your Open World towns;
  • settings: sound, music and graphics quality.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, by uninstalling the app, or, on the website, by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Open World towns can also be deleted one by one from the world list in the game.

The game does not use cookies.

Third-party services

The game itself sends no data anywhere, but two services are involved in delivering it to you:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages, website only). The Android app has the game files built in. On the website, the game's files are served by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when you load the game, and may keep it in server logs for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The game's fonts are loaded from Google's font servers, which receive your IP address and browser information when the fonts download. Google states it does not use this to build profiles. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

There are no advertising networks, analytics tools, social media plugins or in-app purchases in the game.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All game data stays on your device and is under your control as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page: github.com/GukaShin/mobfall-privacy/issues.
